CROSS-BORDER STRUCTURING · P. 14

Permanent establishment

Work carried out in Johor can create a taxable presence. Clearly separate Singapore and Johor activities, and keep careful records.

↔
RELATED-PARTY TRANSACTIONS · P. 14

Transfer pricing

Prices between related companies should follow the arm’s length principle. Define each entity’s role and keep supporting records.

⌁
PEOPLE & MOBILITY · P. 17

Workdays and personal tax

The JS-SEZ does not override domestic tax rules. Track where employees work, their workdays and activities in Johor, and check immigration requirements.

Source: SMF JS-SEZ Playbook, pp. 3, 10, 14 and 17.

04 / Plan your people

Passes for staff and specialists.

The right route depends on the person’s role and activities. Easier cross-border movement does not remove immigration or tax obligations.

01 / EMPLOYMENT

Employment Pass

MIDA assesses applications for expatriate posts. Eligible new and existing companies in manufacturing, selected services and approved regional establishments can apply for MIDA expatriate posts and Employment Pass support letters through Xpats Gateway.

PLAYBOOK · P. 28
02 / SHORT-TERM

Professional Visit Pass

For temporary professional work such as training, consultancy or machine installation on behalf of an overseas company. The Playbook says it can be granted for up to 12 months, is not extendable and does not provide long-term employment rights.
